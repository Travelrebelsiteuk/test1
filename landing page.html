Bonus Points System

Earn Rebel Points with every booking and unlock exclusive rewards for your agency!

How to Earn:

  • 10 points per £100 booked (e.g., a £2,400 package earns 240 points).
  • Bonus 50 points for every 5 bookings in a month.
  • Double points during special promotional campaigns.

Redeem Points:

  • 500 points = £25 cash voucher.
  • 1,000 points = £50 cash voucher.
  • Points can be applied as discounts on future bookings.
